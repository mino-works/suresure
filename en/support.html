How to play

Touch the screen to start. Slide your finger left and right and the car follows. You have 5 hearts and no time limit.

Pass close to the cars ahead for Near Miss, closer for Close!, and the tightest for a gold Perfect!. The closer you pass, the farther you go and the more points you score. Points turn into coins.

Hit a car and you lose a heart. At 0 the run ends, unless you choose Continue to keep going with 2 hearts after a video (once per run).

Need a break? Tap the round button at the top right to pause. Resume, or end the run and keep the coins you earned.

Stages

How many stages are there?

Seven: Road, Ramps, Water, Jungle, Deep Sea, Sky and Space. Each one adds its own rule. Stages you have not opened show as ?? on the title screen.

How do I open the next stage?

Drive in your newest stage. When you have gone far enough, a gate appears on the road. Drive through it to open the next stage. A very long single run can bring the gate early, and your first run of each day counts double distance. Space opens from a gate in Sky.

What are stars and constellations?

Each stage gives up to 3 stars for total distance, and each star gives a free gacha spin. After all 21 stars, driving lights up constellation stars, each with another free spin.

Cars and powers

Are cars different?

Every car has a power. Tap powers such as Nitro, Big Jump, Slow-Mo, Autopilot and Double Points charge as you make close passes; when the button at the bottom right glows, tap it. Auto powers such as Shield, Revive, Magnet and Light work on their own. Getting the same car again raises its level and makes its power stronger, up to Lv5.

You can open every stage with the cars you start with.

Gacha and coins

Where is the gacha?

Title screen > Garage > Gacha. 1 spin costs 150 coins, 10 spins cost 1,500. Every spin gives an item. In a 10-spin, if the first 9 have no SR or better, the 10th is SR or better.

Free spins: your first 10 spins (one SSR guaranteed), your first finished run each day, every stage or constellation star, when new cars are added, and one optional video a day.

Can I buy coins?

Yes. Tap Buy Coins next to your coin count on the gacha screen. Packs: 450, 1,500 and 3,200 coins. Your first 1,500-coin purchase on this device includes 1,500 bonus coins. Prices are shown in your local currency.

Bought and earned coins are counted separately; earned coins are used first. Coins never expire.

What is the Starter Pack?

A one-time pack: 1,500 coins plus one SSR car you pick yourself, not random. It stays under the 10-spin button until you buy it, with no time limit. From the day after you install, it also appears once on the result screen.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

If I delete the app, do I keep my coins?

No. Coins and Starter Pack items live only on your device. Restore Purchases brings back No Ads only. When you change phones, restore from a device backup.

How do I stop my child from buying?

On iPhone, go to Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases and set In-app Purchases to Don't Allow.

Ads and No Ads

When do ads appear?

Never while you drive. A full-screen ad may appear after every 3 runs when you tap Retry, at least 2 minutes apart, up to 6 a day, and never on your first day. Videos play only when you choose them.

How do I remove ads?

Buy No Ads in Settings. Full-screen ads go away, and video rewards such as double coins, test drives, the daily gacha spin and Continue work without videos, with the same limits. After reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.

What is the tracking prompt?

It asks whether ads can be tailored to you. The game works the same if you say no. Change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Other
