How to play

Touch the screen to start. Slide your finger left and right and the car follows. You have 5 hearts and no time limit. Your first run pauses once to show the goal and how to earn coins in two short lines.

Pass close to the cars ahead for Near Miss, closer for Close!, and the tightest for a gold Perfect!. The closer you pass, the farther you go and the more points you score. Points turn into coins.

Hit a car and you lose a heart. At 0 the run ends. On the result screen, Continue here lets you watch a video and keep going with 2 hearts, as many times as you like in a run. It is not offered on your first run after install, after a clear or after you quit a run. A 3, 2, 1 countdown starts the run again.

Need a break? Tap the round button at the top right to pause.

Stages

How many stages are there?

15. You drive through 7 worlds, Road, Jump Park, Water, Jungle, Under the Sea, Sky and Space, across 15 stages. Each world adds its own rule, and later stages add night, fog, storms and meteor showers.

How do I clear a stage?

Reach the gate in one run to clear the stage and open the next one. The gate is 5 km away in Stage 1 and 22 km in Stage 15. A bar on the right always shows how far the gate is.

You never need a video to reach any stage. On a stage you have not cleared, the gate moves closer as your distance there adds up, and after a set number of runs of 30 seconds or more it comes to 95% of your best run there.

What are stars and constellations?

Your score on a clear earns 1 to 3 stars for that stage. Each new star gives a free gacha pull. Clear all 15 stages to start lighting up constellations.

Cars and powers

Are cars different?

Every car has a power. Tap powers such as Nitro, Warp, Big Jump, Slow-Mo, Autopilot and Double Points charge as you make close passes; when the button at the bottom right glows, tap it. Auto powers such as Shield, Revive, Magnet and Light work on their own. Getting the same car again raises its level and makes its power stronger, up to Lv5.

You can open every stage with the car you start with.

What is paint?

From version 1.9.6, the gacha has two kinds of items: cars and paints. There are 30 paints. In the Garage, open Paint to paint one of your cars. Each paint job uses 1 paint. The car remembers the color, so you can switch between its original color and any color it has had, any time, for free. Paint changes only the look, not the driving or the score.

Where did car parts and colors go?

In version 1.9.6 the cars became 3D. With that change, look parts such as stickers and the colors bought with coins were removed. Use paint to change a car's color. Your cars, car levels, selected car, coins and pity count stay the same.

I cannot find the car Leaper.

In version 1.9.6 the SSR car Leaper was renamed Hopper. If you owned it, you now own Hopper, with the same level and power.

Gacha and coins

Where is the gacha?

Tap Gacha at the bottom of the title screen. 1 pull costs 150 coins, 10 pulls cost 1,500. Every pull gives an item. Every 10-pull has 1 SR or better car guaranteed: if the first 9 have none, the 10th is an SR or better car. This also applies to a 10-pull mixed with free pulls.

Free pulls include your first 10 pulls (one SSR car guaranteed), each new star, and one pull every 7 days you finish a run, with an SR or better car guaranteed. Every pull counts toward the 50-pull pity.

Can I buy coins?

Yes. Tap Buy Coins next to your coin count on the gacha screen. Packs: 450, 1,500 and 3,200 coins. Your first 1,500-coin purchase on this device includes 1,500 bonus coins. Prices are shown in your local currency.

Bought and earned coins are counted separately; earned coins are used first. Coins never expire.

What is the Starter Pack?

A one-time pack: 1,500 coins plus one SSR car you pick yourself, not random. It stays under the 10-spin button until you buy it, with no time limit. From the day after you install, it also appears once on the result screen.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

If I delete the app, do I keep my coins?

No. Coins and Starter Pack items live only on your device. Restore Purchases brings back No Ads only. When you change phones, restore from a device backup.

How do I stop my child from buying?

On iPhone, go to Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases and set In-app Purchases to Don't Allow.

Ads and No Ads

When do ads appear?

Never while you drive. A full-screen ad may appear after every 3 runs, right after a run ends and before the result, or after a clear when you tap Next. Ads are at least 60 seconds apart, up to 6 a day, and never before you first clear Stage 2 or on the day you install. They are skipped in some cases, such as when a run ended near the gate. Videos play only when you choose them.

How do I remove ads?

Buy No Ads in Settings. Full-screen ads go away, and video rewards such as double coins, 150 coins at the gacha and Continue here work without videos, with the same limits. After reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.

What is the tracking prompt?

It asks whether ads can be tailored to you. The game works the same if you say no. Change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Other

Can I play offline?

Yes. Ads and video rewards just will not appear. Buying coins needs a connection.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the gear on the title screen.
