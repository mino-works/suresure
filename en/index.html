Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026. Updated October 10, 2026. / 日本語

This policy explains how mino works ("we") handles information in the app "Suresure" ("the app").

Update of October 7, 2026: version 1.5 adds stages, the gacha, car abilities, the Starter Pack and English. Sections 2, 6 and 7 were updated. Version 1.5.3 removes the Starter Pack time limit.

Update of October 10, 2026: version 1.5.6 adds 4 space cars to the gacha. Section 2 now lists the day you first opened 1.5.6 and whether its free spin was received. Nothing new is sent off the device.

1. What we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information. There is no account, no login and no server of ours. We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools.

To keep the app free, it includes Google AdMob by Google LLC, which collects the information described in section 6.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Run records: best score, distance per stage and best distance, number of runs, records per car
  • Unlocked stages, stage stars, constellation progress, dates of your first run of the day (60 days), whether you have seen stage intros and test drives
  • Coins, with bought and earned coins counted separately; coins bought and used; total amount paid; whether the first-purchase bonus was used; purchase transaction IDs (last 50, to avoid adding coins twice)
  • Starter Pack: whether it was shown on the result screen, whether you bought it, and whether an SSR car is still to be picked
  • Cars and items you own, car levels, the look, color and car you selected
  • Gacha counters: spins toward the guaranteed SSR, the SSR you picked, whether the first 10 spins were used, dates of free spins (60 days), the day you first opened 1.5.6 and whether its free spin was received (to show a NEW mark on the new cars for 14 days and to give that free spin only once)
  • Install date and whether you have seen tips and notices
  • Ad counters to limit how often ads appear
  • Whether you have seen the tracking explanation
  • Sound and vibration settings, and whether you bought No Ads
  • A backup copy of this data in case it gets corrupted

Data from older versions, such as Today's Road and missions, may remain on the device unused. Your device's own backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include this data.

3. Deleting data

Deleting the app deletes all of this data. Bought coins and items from the Starter Pack cannot be recovered after you delete the app, because they exist only on your device. No Ads can be restored with Restore Purchases in Settings. When you change phones, set up the new one from a backup.

4. Device features

The app does not access your photos, camera, microphone, contacts or GPS location.

5. Notifications

The app does not use notifications.

6. Ads

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. No ads appear while you are driving. There are no banner ads.

  • Full-screen ads: after every 3 runs when you tap Retry on the result screen, at least 2 minutes apart, up to 6 a day. Never on your first day and never before the first run after opening the app.
  • Optional videos you choose to watch: double coins (5 a day), a test drive of the next car (1 a day), one gacha spin (1 a day), stage test drives (from the second one, 1 a day), and Continue with 2 hearts when you run out (once per run).

After a run where you watched a video or continued, the next full-screen ad is skipped. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (G).

AdMob may collect your advertising ID and app-specific identifiers, IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), ad interactions, device and OS information, and diagnostic data, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Personalized ads

  • On iPhone, the first time you open the app, a short explanation is followed by the system tracking prompt. Personalized ads using the IDFA are shown only if you allow tracking. The game works the same either way. Change it any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent tools.
  • If you buy No Ads, full-screen ads and the tracking prompt are not shown.

Report an inappropriate ad from the [i] mark on the ad, and please let us know too. Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ad settings: https://myadcenter.google.com/

7. In-app purchases

You can open every stage just by driving, and coins come from every run. We sell:

  • No Ads (one-time purchase): removes full-screen ads and gives video rewards without videos, with the same limits.
  • Coins: 450, 1,500 and 3,200. Your first 1,500-coin purchase on the device includes 1,500 bonus coins. Coins are used for the gacha and cars, and never expire.
  • Starter Pack (one time only): 1,500 coins plus one SSR car you pick yourself. Shown on the gacha screen until you buy it, with no time limit.

Gacha results are random. The odds are shown above the spin buttons, in Full Odds, and on the support page.

Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Purchase results are recorded only on your device.
